All cutscenes

The WebGL build asks for cutscenes through a file-existence check that cannot succeed in a browser, so it falls back to an embedded clip that WebGL cannot draw. The clips play here instead.
Video files: 1080p VP8 / Vorbis WebM, the exact format the game itself uses.
